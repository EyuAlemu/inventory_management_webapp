# Standard User Guide

This guide explains how to use the app as a standard `user`. Standard users use a personal stock workflow: receive stock into their own account, sell from personal stock, return leftover items to inventory, take items out, and review their own activity.

## Dashboard

Use **Overview** to review your personal stock and activity.

1. Log in with your user account.
2. Click **Overview**.
3. Review current stock.
4. Review received and sold quantities.
5. Review recent activity.

## Scan QR / Barcode

Use **Scan QR / Barcode** when you want to scan an item code with your camera.

1. Click **Scan QR / Barcode**.
2. Allow browser camera access if prompted.
3. Center the QR code or barcode in the camera image.
4. Capture the image.
5. If the code is detected, the app opens **Receive Stock** with the item code prefilled.

## Receive Stock

Use **Receive Stock** to add stock from system inventory into your personal account.

1. Click **Receive Stock**.
2. Select a location.
3. Select an available item.
4. Optional: enter or scan an item code.
5. Click **View Item Details**.
6. Review system quantity and your current quantity.
7. Enter **Quantity to Receive**.
8. Click **Add to My Stock**.

The app reduces system inventory and increases your personal stock.

## Return Leftover Items

Use **Return to Inventory** when you have leftover stock that should go back to inventory.

1. Click **Receive Stock**.
2. Select the item you already have in your personal stock.
3. Click **View Item Details**.
4. Under **User Account Actions**, enter **Return Leftover Quantity**.
5. Click **Return to Inventory**.

The app reduces your personal stock and adds the quantity back to inventory.

## Take Items Out

Use **Take Out** when stock should leave your account without being returned to inventory.

1. Click **Receive Stock**.
2. Select the item.
3. Click **View Item Details**.
4. Under **User Account Actions**, enter **Take Out Quantity**.
5. Click **Take Out**.

The app reduces your personal stock but does not add the quantity back to inventory.

## Sell Item

Use **Create Sale** to sell from your personal stock.

1. Click **Create Sale**.
2. Enter item code.
3. Click **View Sale Details**.
4. Review your available quantity.
5. Enter quantity sold.
6. Review sale preview.
7. Click **Save Sale**.

The app reduces your personal stock and records a sale transaction.

## Activity Logs

Use **Activity Logs** to review your personal stock history.

1. Click **Activity Logs**.
2. Search by item code if needed.
3. Review action:
   - `Added to My Stock`
   - `Sold Item`
   - `Returned to Inventory`
   - `Taken Out`
4. Review quantity before, quantity changed, and quantity after.
5. Confirm verification when available.

## Detailed Walkthrough: Receive, Sell, and Return Leftovers

### Step 1: Receive Stock

1. Click **Receive Stock**.
2. Select a location.
3. Select an available item.
4. Click **View Item Details**.
5. Enter quantity to receive.
6. Click **Add to My Stock**.

### Step 2: Sell Some Stock

1. Click **Create Sale**.
2. Enter the item code.
3. Click **View Sale Details**.
4. Enter quantity sold.
5. Click **Save Sale**.

### Step 3: Return Leftover Stock

1. Click **Receive Stock**.
2. Select the same item.
3. Click **View Item Details**.
4. Enter **Return Leftover Quantity**.
5. Click **Return to Inventory**.

### Step 4: Review History

1. Click **Activity Logs**.
2. Search for the item code.
3. Confirm receive, sale, and return actions appear.
4. Confirm verification is `Verified` where available.
